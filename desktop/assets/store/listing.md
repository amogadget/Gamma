## Description

Gamma PDF is a reading and note-taking app for research papers. Open a PDF,
highlight it, and keep your notes as an outliner where every highlight is a
block you can nest, link, search, and share.

Everything is stored on your own computer, in a local workspace on your
disk. If you also run a Gamma server (on a NAS or a VPS), the app opens it
as a second workspace and switches between them from the toolbar.

READ AND ANNOTATE
• Open a paper by dropping in a PDF or pasting a link (arXiv, DOI, or a
  publisher page). Gamma finds the PDF, and looks for a legal open-access
  copy when the DOI is paywalled.
• Select text or drag a box around a figure, pick a color, and add a
  comment. Each highlight becomes a note block, and clicking the block jumps
  the PDF back to it.
• Highlights already saved in the file by other PDF readers are imported as
  blocks too.
• Citations in the PDF are clickable: jump to the reference, and fetch a
  cited arXiv or DOI paper into your library with one click.
• On open, the title, authors, and venue are resolved automatically. One
  click copies BibTeX or a slide-ready citation.

TAKE NOTES
• Highlights and free notes are the same kind of block, so a paper's notes
  and a plain page are edited the same way: Enter for a new block, Tab to
  nest, drag to reorder, one undo history for the whole page.
• Live preview while you type: Markdown, LaTeX math ($…$ and $$…$$), code
  blocks, callouts, and tables render in place while the block you are on
  stays raw. Math gets bracket coloring and \command autocomplete.
• Link pages with [[page]] mentions and embed blocks with ![[block]] embeds
  that edit the source in place.
• Paste images and tables straight into a note. Tables are edited cell by
  cell, and images can be captioned and resized in place.

ORGANIZE AND SEARCH
• Labels are flat tags (an author, a keyword) that filter with one click.
• Folders are a topic hierarchy built from the paths you use, such as
  qc/neutral-atom. One paper can live in several folders.
• Search finds titles, notes, highlights, and the full text of every PDF at
  once, with match-case, whole-word, and regex options. Opening a hit lights
  the matches up on the page.

ASK AN AI ABOUT YOUR PAPERS (optional)
• Chat about the open paper, or several at once, and ask the assistant to
  explain a passage, summarize, or compare. It can also tidy your library:
  list, read, and search your papers, rename them, and file them into
  folders, with every step shown as it runs.
• AI features are off until you add your own provider in Settings (an
  Anthropic or OpenAI API key, or a ChatGPT sign-in). Nothing is sent to any
  AI service otherwise.

SHARE AND MOVE YOUR DATA
• Share an annotated paper by link, with view or edit rights per person.
• Import Logseq PDF exports and Zotero libraries with their annotations, and
  Markdown or Notion exports as notes.
• Export a zip of all your data at any time and restore it on another
  computer or server.
• The Gamma Connector browser extension (a separate download) saves the
  paper you are reading into Gamma from the arXiv, DOI, or publisher tab.

Gamma is open source: https://github.com/tim4431/Gamma

## Product features (Partner Center bullets, one per line)

- Highlight PDFs: text or box highlights, colors, and comments, each stored as a note block
- Outliner notes with live Markdown and LaTeX preview, code blocks, callouts, and tables
- Open papers by arXiv, DOI, or publisher link; metadata, BibTeX, and citations resolved automatically
- Clickable citations: jump to the reference and fetch the cited paper in one click
- Labels and topic folders to organize your library, one paper in several folders
- Search notes, highlights, and the full text of every PDF at once
- Optional AI chat about your papers using your own Anthropic, OpenAI, or ChatGPT account
- Share an annotated paper by link with view or edit rights
- Import from Logseq and Zotero; export everything as a zip
- Local workspaces on your disk, plus any Gamma server you host as another workspace
