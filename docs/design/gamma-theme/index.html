III. Coherence and collective dynamics

Understanding the lifetime of collective states is central to building reliable quantum systems. Repeated measurements reveal how the coherence changes with the trapping geometry.

The scattering rate sets the decay of the collective excitation. We compare these measurements across the two configurations and recover the expected exponential behavior.

N(t) = N₀e−Γt

These observations connect the measured lifetime to the underlying interactions. The result provides a useful reference for the next series of experiments.
